## About me

I work in governance, risk and compliance at a global cybersecurity product company, where I split my time between two things:

- **In-house security & GRC** — co-owning the ISMS and compliance program for a multi-product SaaS suite across India, UK, US and Middle East entities: SOC 2 Type II, third-party risk, customer security questionnaires, awareness, change management and BCP/DR.
- **Client delivery** — independently running ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR/DPDPA and NIST programs for clients, and representing them in front of certification bodies and regulators.

I also act as a **vCISO** for a US-based SaaS company, owning their security governance, NIST CSF program and customer due-diligence process.

Before this, I spent a year on the offensive side doing web VAPT, which still shapes how I think about controls: a policy is only as good as the thing it actually stops.
